How to play

Two of the same — tap a tile, then tap another with the same picture. They clear away together.

Any two Elvis tiles go together, even when the photographs are different. They are the easy ones.

Bright tiles can be played. Dim tiles are stuck — something is on top of them, or they are hemmed in on both sides.
